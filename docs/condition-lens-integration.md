Scope [#160](https://github.com/uobnnisiuk/footprint-sim/issues/160), Work-Key `FP-24-CONDITION-SEARCH-LENSES-V1`. This is **not** a claim that the original conditional relations are physical encounter laws or that all 24 have been implemented. The original `Paṭṭhāna` Paccayuddeso/Paccayaniddeso is [transcribed here](https://www.aimwell.org/patthana.html); compare the [Abhidhammattha Saṅgaha, chapter 8](https://www.ruyimeditation.org/theravada/abhidhamma/paccaya-sangaha/) and [commentary list](https://orthodoxtheravada.org/lists/twenty-four-conditional-relations/). **09** is a *diagnostic leave-one-received-source-root-out* question, not a claim of causal strength, reliability, or physical movement. For the same target Person, tick, cutoff, seed and public model, it drops all eligible receipts belonging to one already-received ExternalSource ancestry root and uses the existing receipt builder and M1-D/E Compare. It reports each root's removed received original IDs, each method's before/after outer, preferred, uncertain, conflicted, source-group count and added/removed outer candidates. An unknown ancestry is not counted as an independent root. Two independently received same-tick Person roots are required for a 09 question. An existing method must show either strict outer widening on root removal, or a strict reduction in conflicted candidates (without newly conflicted candidates) together with outer shrinkage. Widening retains its original question ID and reason; conflict relief uses the explicit `conflicted-outcome-depends-on-received-independent-person-root` reason. Mixed cases are normalized to one 09 question with all relevant received roots, preferring the widening reason. Merely asking does not alter the actual comparison row. B-eligible is the original widening positive ({a,b}, {b,c}); K4-nested ({b}, {a,b}) also widens, while K2-conflict ({a}, {c}) and K3-conflict ({a,b}, {c}) exercise conflict relief; B-none/future/offtick, S3 same-root copies, S4 and R redundant roots, Device-only and missing receipts are negative cases. All resulting values remain finite synthetic outputs, not real-world evidence reliability or rescue success.

**02** asks whether Device evidence actually concerns Person P, not an ontological derivation from ārammaṇa. **04** uses anantara only as a bounded analogy to notice that received Confirmed Person evidence at tick t+1 does **not** establish the Person at target tick t; it asks for direct evidence at t or a *declared* public transition without inventing backward movement. **06** asks for a genuinely independent second same-tick Person ancestry even when the single existing ancestry already narrows outer to one region (AR-83). Same-tick coincidence, copied originals, and candidate count do not demonstrate independence or spatial causality. Do not infer that an unknown relation is absent. None of these questions narrows a candidate set. Unlike #106, they do not discover an unconfirmed public path; unlike #112, they do not repeat the B→D fixed relation fixture. PRs #168, #169, #170 and #171 have independent LIMITED_PASS, normal merges and post-merge CI. Rev8 main has eight limited lenses (02/04/06/08/09/10/12/22), with 16 unimplemented/unassessed; Rev9 proposes 21 only after its own code review, normal merge and post-merge CI.

**10** is a *bounded question only*, not a forward movement or causal propagation rule. It looks at actually received, Confirmed Person evidence for the same target at tick t−1 when no eligible target-tick Person evidence has been received, and asks for a direct target-tick observation or *declared* forward public reachability. Its question reason is `received-prior-tick-not-target-tick`, distinct from 04's t+1-only question, 06's same-tick source corroboration, 09's source-root ablation diagnostic, and #106's missing declared public path. 11 remains unimplemented; a later observation is not silently treated as proof of the previous tick. E-prior-none, E-prior-eligible and E-prior-future contrast absent, legal and future receipts; F-followup activates 04 but not 10; G-offtick/Device-only are negative controls. Questions cite only already received original and observation IDs plus ExternalSource ancestry and never refer to held-only inputs. Their presence alone does not change any M1-D/E outer/unknown/conflict method row. The Rev5 AR-88 conflict correction was independently reviewed and verified after PR #170 merged.

**12 (Rev6)** is a deliberately limited engineering analogy to āsevana: the original condition concerns the repetition of mental phenomena, not retransmission or physical tracking ([Paṭṭhāna](https://www.aimwell.org/patthana.html), [Paccaya 12](https://epitaka.org/en/book/Pa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhuddes/12-%C4%81sevanapaccayo-110)). If the rescuer has received two different Confirmed Person original observations at ticks t−1 and t, with different original/observation IDs but the same single ExternalSource root, the lens asks whether these are separate observations or copies/re-timestamping. It does not infer continuous presence, or count distinct IDs as independent roots. T-repeat is the positive; T-independent is a changed source-assumption model, not an added answer; T-future-prior/current, T-device, T-gap, T-sametick are negatives; T-copy and T-cutoff check idempotency and the receipt boundary. Only received records are cited. Rev6's nine T cases, eight question modes and schemaVersion 4 were verified by the PR #171 CI and post-merge main CI. General evaluation JSON/CSV remain unchanged.

**08 (Rev7)** applies nissaya only as a bounded *question lens*, not as an equation of a doctrinal condition with electronic source dependence. Two distinct Confirmed Person original/observation IDs at the same target tick have **different claimed single ExternalSource roots**, yet dropping either actually received root leaves **every method's outer, preferred, uncertain and conflicted region sets unchanged**. The lens then asks whether the original observations have independent upstream generation or share an unverified common origin. It does not declare a relationship true or false, discard either record, or change source groups or candidate methods. A different source-root label is **not proof of statistical independence**; identical supported locations are **not proof of copying**. N-redundant is positive; the pre-existing S4 and R-redundant cases are also 08-positive because their two received roots have the same unchanged candidate outcome. Their original fixture inputs and all earlier lens-specific modes remain intact, but their `combined` question sets now each gain the 08 question. In the S1→S4 answer difference, receiving a second claimed root resolves 06's same-tick corroboration request yet creates the 08 upstream-provenance question; different declared roots do not establish independent original observations. These output changes are intentional and directly tested, not byte-identical preservation of every old `combined` output. N-overlap and N-conflict preserve 09 instead; N-same-root, N-copy, N-future, N-device, N-offtick are negative; N-cutoff and N-order check receipt and deterministic ordering. The dedicated Rev7 report proposes schemaVersion 5, nine modes and ten additional cases while retaining the original 35. Rev7 subsequently received independent code review, normal merge and successful main CI; the distinction between source provenance and physical dependence remains. No source root is ablated from real inference.

## Scope Rev8 — 22 natthi: zero received evidence is not Person absence

The Paṭṭhāna's *natthi* concerns the cessation of a preceding mental phenomenon as a condition for a following one. It does **not** establish missing persons, missing received records or physical absence. This new lens is only a deliberately **weak engineering analogy**: when the rescuer's post-gate `ReceivedRecords.Count == 0` at the actual cutoff, ask what legitimate Person evidence could be obtained. The generated question cites Person P, target tick and cutoff, with **empty received original, observation and ancestry IDs**; it may not quote an unreceived held record or a future receipt, and it never alters a candidate, source group or M1-D/E comparison.

The seven additional finite U controls (public `{a,b,c}`, Person P, target tick 5, cutoff 10, two held synthetic originals) explicitly distinguish:
- `U-none`: no received originals; exactly one 22 question and no source IDs
- `U-future`: receipt at tick 11; identical visible inputs, questions and methods to U-none
- `U-eligible`: legal target-tick Person receipt at tick 8; 22 resolves and 06 may be newly asked; the old comparison, not the question, supplies method-specific candidate changes
- `U-device`, `U-followup` and `U-prior`: already received Device-only, Person@6 or Person@4; each suppresses 22 without suppressing legitimate 02, 04 or 10 questions
- `U-duplicate`: repeated receipt of the same original; no second observation, root or extra question

The 45 existing cases and their nine pre-existing modes remain present; seven new cases yield 52, and new mode `22` yields ten modes. The dedicated report is `schemaVersion=6` while the input fixture stays at version 1; general evaluation JSON/CSV contracts are unchanged. Compare `U-none` to `U-future` for zero effect, `U-eligible` for resolved/new questions and method-specific changes, and check the CLI-written JSON plus repeatable output bytes. Do not treat a different synthetic source-assumption model as a new receipt or infer universal improvement, rescue times or real-world correctness.

Changes remain within `ConditionLensAnalysis.cs`, `ConditionLensTests.cs`, `experiments/condition_lenses/fixture.json` and this document. Existing receipt builder, public model, CLI dispatch, SDK, CI and permissions are untouched. **Rev8 is not an integrated eighth lens until a separate independent code review, current-head CI, protected normal merge and post-merge main checks succeed**. Rev7 (#172) was already integrated and verified. The outstanding #78 acceptance-condition-8 comparison of 7 scenarios × 4 artifacts against original Base bytes and AR-86 truth-exclusion safety issue are independent open items and are not waived by Rev8 success.

## Scope Rev9 — 21 atthi: a received observation can be temporally inapplicable

In the Paṭṭhāna, *atthi-paccaya* concerns the presence of mental/material phenomena as a condition, **not** Person presence, location, or arbitrary time extrapolation. This is a weak, bounded engineering analogy: when a rescuer has received Confirmed exact-tick Person evidence only at times at least two ticks away from the target tick, and none at the target or either adjacent tick, ask for legitimately received target-tick Person evidence or an explicit declared public temporal applicability relation. The dedicated question reason is `received-distant-person-observation-not-target-tick`. It includes only received original/observation IDs, ExternalSource roots, observed ticks and cutoff. It does not infer movement, presence, absence or eligibility from a held or future observation.

The H group holds exactly two synthetic originals, with Person P, target tick 5, cutoff 10 and public regions `{a,b,c}`: A is a Person observation at tick 7, regions `{a,c}`, legitimately received at tick 8, root `root-h-distant`; B is Person@5 `{b}`, root `root-h-target`. H-none receives only A and must ask 21 (not 04, 10 or 22); H-eligible receives B at tick 9, resolves 21 and asks existing 06; H-future sets B receipt to tick 11, leaving the visible evidence, every old question mode and all four method outputs unchanged; H-duplicate retransmits the same A receipt, not a second independent root. D-offtick and G-offtick are pre-existing distant-tick positive controls; T-gap, adjacent-tick, target-tick, zero-receipt and Device-only cases are negatives. Receiving B may narrow the existing M1-D/E output; the question never does.

This branch retains all 52 earlier cases and their ten earlier modes, adds four H cases and one independent `21` mode (56 cases / 11 modes), and reports dedicated schemaVersion 7 while preserving fixture schemaVersion 1 and the existing general JSON/CSV contracts. The actual CLI-saved report must verify the 21→06 handoff, both unchanged controls, 4 method-specific outer/unknown/conflict/source-group results, quoted received IDs only, and deterministic repeat bytes. A new legitimate receipt is a change in information, **not** evidence of superior search methodology. No new physical/temporal propagation, source credibility or real-world rescue claim is licensed.
