# 24 paccaya as *question lenses* — bounded prototype

Scope [#160](https://github.com/uobnnisiuk/footprint-sim/issues/160), Work-Key `FP-24-CONDITION-SEARCH-LENSES-V1`. This is **not** a claim that the original conditional relations are physical encounter laws or that all 24 have been implemented. The original `Paṭṭhāna` Paccayuddeso/Paccayaniddeso is [transcribed here](https://www.aimwell.org/patthana.html); compare the [Abhidhammattha Saṅgaha, chapter 8](https://www.ruyimeditation.org/theravada/abhidhamma/paccaya-sangaha/) and [commentary list](https://orthodoxtheravada.org/lists/twenty-four-conditional-relations/). **09** is a *diagnostic leave-one-received-source-root-out* question, not a claim of causal strength, reliability, or physical movement. For the same target Person, tick, cutoff, seed and public model, it drops all eligible receipts belonging to one already-received ExternalSource ancestry root and uses the existing receipt builder and M1-D/E Compare. It reports each root's removed received original IDs, each method's before/after outer, preferred, uncertain, conflicted, source-group count and added/removed outer candidates. An unknown ancestry is not counted as an independent root. Two independently received same-tick Person roots are required for a 09 question. An existing method must show either strict outer widening on root removal, or a strict reduction in conflicted candidates (without newly conflicted candidates) together with outer shrinkage. Widening retains its original question ID and reason; conflict relief uses the explicit `conflicted-outcome-depends-on-received-independent-person-root` reason. Mixed cases are normalized to one 09 question with all relevant received roots, preferring the widening reason. Merely asking does not alter the actual comparison row. B-eligible is the original widening positive ({a,b}, {b,c}); K4-nested ({b}, {a,b}) also widens, while K2-conflict ({a}, {c}) and K3-conflict ({a,b}, {c}) exercise conflict relief; B-none/future/offtick, S3 same-root copies, S4 and R redundant roots, Device-only and missing receipts are negative cases. All resulting values remain finite synthetic outputs, not real-world evidence reliability or rescue success.

**02** asks whether Device evidence actually concerns Person P, not an ontological derivation from ārammaṇa. **04** uses anantara only as a bounded analogy to notice that received Confirmed Person evidence at tick t+1 does **not** establish the Person at target tick t; it asks for direct evidence at t or a *declared* public transition without inventing backward movement. **06** asks for a genuinely independent second same-tick Person ancestry even when the single existing ancestry already narrows outer to one region (AR-83). Same-tick coincidence, copied originals, and candidate count do not demonstrate independence or spatial causality. Do not infer that an unknown relation is absent. None of these questions narrows a candidate set. Unlike #106, they do not discover an unconfirmed public path; unlike #112, they do not repeat the B→D fixed relation fixture. PRs #168, #169, #170 and #171 have independent LIMITED_PASS, normal merges and post-merge CI. Verified main has six limited lenses (02/04/06/09/10/12) and 18 unimplemented/unassessed; Rev7 adds 08 only after its own code review, normal merge and post-merge CI.

**10** is a *bounded question only*, not a forward movement or causal propagation rule. It looks at actually received, Confirmed Person evidence for the same target at tick t−1 when no eligible target-tick Person evidence has been received, and asks for a direct target-tick observation or *declared* forward public reachability. Its question reason is `received-prior-tick-not-target-tick`, distinct from 04's t+1-only question, 06's same-tick source corroboration, 09's source-root ablation diagnostic, and #106's missing declared public path. 11 remains unimplemented; a later observation is not silently treated as proof of the previous tick. E-prior-none, E-prior-eligible and E-prior-future contrast absent, legal and future receipts; F-followup activates 04 but not 10; G-offtick/Device-only are negative controls. Questions cite only already received original and observation IDs plus ExternalSource ancestry and never refer to held-only inputs. Their presence alone does not change any M1-D/E outer/unknown/conflict method row. The Rev5 AR-88 conflict correction was independently reviewed and verified after PR #170 merged.

**12 (Rev6)** is a deliberately limited engineering analogy to āsevana: the original condition concerns the repetition of mental phenomena, not retransmission or physical tracking ([Paṭṭhāna](https://www.aimwell.org/patthana.html), [Paccaya 12](https://epitaka.org/en/book/Pa%E1%B9%AD%E1%B9%ADhuddes/12-%C4%81sevanapaccayo-110)). If the rescuer has received two different Confirmed Person original observations at ticks t−1 and t, with different original/observation IDs but the same single ExternalSource root, the lens asks whether these are separate observations or copies/re-timestamping. It does not infer continuous presence, or count distinct IDs as independent roots. T-repeat is the positive; T-independent is a changed source-assumption model, not an added answer; T-future-prior/current, T-device, T-gap, T-sametick are negatives; T-copy and T-cutoff check idempotency and the receipt boundary. Only received records are cited. Rev6's nine T cases, eight question modes and schemaVersion 4 were verified by the PR #171 CI and post-merge main CI. General evaluation JSON/CSV remain unchanged.

**08 (Rev7)** applies nissaya only as a bounded *question lens*, not as an equation of a doctrinal condition with electronic source dependence. Two distinct Confirmed Person original/observation IDs at the same target tick have **different claimed single ExternalSource roots**, yet dropping either actually received root leaves **every method's outer, preferred, uncertain and conflicted region sets unchanged**. The lens then asks whether the original observations have independent upstream generation or share an unverified common origin. It does not declare a relationship true or false, discard either record, or change source groups or candidate methods. A different source-root label is **not proof of statistical independence**; identical supported locations are **not proof of copying**. N-redundant is positive; the pre-existing S4 and R-redundant cases are also 08-positive because their two received roots have the same unchanged candidate outcome. Their original fixture inputs and all earlier lens-specific modes remain intact, but their `combined` question sets now each gain the 08 question. In the S1→S4 answer difference, receiving a second claimed root resolves 06's same-tick corroboration request yet creates the 08 upstream-provenance question; different declared roots do not establish independent original observations. These output changes are intentional and directly tested, not byte-identical preservation of every old `combined` output. N-overlap and N-conflict preserve 09 instead; N-same-root, N-copy, N-future, N-device, N-offtick are negative; N-cutoff and N-order check receipt and deterministic ordering. The dedicated Rev7 report proposes schemaVersion 5, nine modes and ten additional cases while retaining the original 35. This branch is **not an integrated seventh lens** until independent code review, CI, usual merge and post-merge CI. No source root is ablated from real inference.

## Reproduce the finite synthetic contrast

```bash
dotnet run --project src/FootprintSim.Cli -- evaluate-condition-lenses \
  --fixture experiments/condition_lenses/fixture.json \
  --output results/condition-lenses
```

Output: `results/condition-lenses/condition-lenses.json`. Forty-five named cases retain all 35 Rev6 cases and add ten N controls. Earlier cases include **A** (Device-only and later Person receipt), **B** (two distinct same-tick Person originals, with intersection `{b}` only when both are eligible), **C** (02/06 together and duplicate receipt), **D** (04: t+1 evidence), **S** (06: independent same-tick evidence), **E/F/G** (10: prior versus following tick), **K** (09: conflict root removal), and **T** (12: adjacent distinct observations with shared roots). **N** tests unknown upstream provenance despite unchanged method regions. All are finite public synthetic input, target tick 5, cutoff 10, seed 7, public regions `{a,b,c}`, at most two held originals. A future receipt at tick 11 and never-received inputs cannot be cited in any question.

Each case exposes the old #106 diagnostic, nine question modes (none, 02, 04, 06, 08, 09, 10, 12, combined), and the same unchanged M1-D/E method rows per mode. The dedicated condition-lenses report on this branch is schemaVersion 5 (adding 08); fixture schemaVersion stays 1 and the general evaluation JSON/CSV contracts remain unchanged. Both root-removal results are retained with each method's before/after outer, preferred, uncertain, conflicted regions and source groups. Deterministic byte-identical CLI output from the same fixture and seed is required. Legal new receipts, future receipts, unchanged effects and worsening outcomes remain distinguishable; question generation alone never narrows candidates.

The model has no hazard-transition declarations, and the #106 diagnostic is therefore unavailable rather than proof of absent public relations. Same-tick Person projection uses the existing builder/reconstructor. Rev4 E/F/G, Rev5 K and Rev6 T cases were verified in the respective PR and integration CI. Rev7 N controls are *predeclared acceptance expectations* until this branch's current-head CI and independent code review confirm them. Synthetic method outputs do not measure rescue benefit, physical causality, or the effectiveness of 24-paccaya as a real-world model. `PersonPersonEncounter` is a declared simulated person-evidence relation; a device-only original is never promoted. Legacy inclusive interval and traversal-enabled problems remain outside this point-tick experiment.

No new data, model, SDK, truth-side history or live-device access is involved. Do not commit generated `results/`. Rev7's scope approval is separate from still-required current-head CI, independent code review, usual protected merge and post-merge main CI. Issue #160 and UOB-85 remain open until all 24 conditions have individually audited implementation/validation or justified independent non-applicability. #78 AC8's 28 byte comparisons and the AR-86 safety counterexample are unresolved.
